5 Star Clips Publisher - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

5 Star Clips Publisher is a private desktop tool used only by the operator of the 5 Star Clips channels to publish clips to the operator's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other people.

What data the app handles

  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok when the operator authorizes the app. They are stored only on the operator's own computer, outside any shared folder, and are used solely to call TikTok's APIs on the operator's behalf.
  • Basic account info (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) and the creator settings returned by TikTok's Content Posting API, used only to confirm the right account is connected and which privacy options are allowed.
  • Publish IDs and post status returned by TikTok, stored locally so the same clip is never sent twice.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the operator's computer until the operator revokes access, either in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the local token file. Revoking access stops all API calls immediately.
