5 Star Clips Publisher - Terms of Service

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What this app is

5 Star Clips Publisher is a private desktop tool used by the operator of the 5 Star Clips channels to publish short gaming video clips to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-ups.

2. Who may use it

Only the channel operator, using the operator's own TikTok account, which the operator connects through TikTok's official Login Kit. No other TikTok users are served by this app.

3. What the app does with TikTok

  • Uses TikTok Login Kit so the operator can authorize the app (user.info.basic).
  • Uses the Content Posting API to send approved video clips to the operator's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload) or to post them to the operator's own profile (video.publish).
  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by the operator before it is sent.

4. Content

The operator is responsible for every clip published and confirms they own it or have permission from the rights holder. Clips must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Gameplay footage remains the property of its owners; this app claims no rights over it.

The app is provided as-is for the operator's own use, without warranties of any kind.
